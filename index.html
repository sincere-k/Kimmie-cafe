Kimmie is the owner of the café. Open from 8PM - 2AM.

Our Story.

Kimmie really likes iced lattes and matcha, especially cold-whisked matcha. He also likes different foods and drinks. He wanted to share these interests with people while creating a safe place for everyone to enjoy themselves, with friends or alone. Whether they're drinking, chilling, studying, or hanging out with friends, everyone is welcome. He especially likes staying out late at night, so he created a place where people can relax during the nighttime, with a taxi service upfront so people can get home safely.

Come visit us and enjoy our drinks at night! And stay safe!! :3
